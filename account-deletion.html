Delete your Sunset Chaser account

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

Delete your account in the app

  1. Open Sunset Chaser and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open the Account tab.
  3. Choose Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

What is deleted

Deleting an account permanently removes the account profile, saved locations and schedules, push-notification tokens, community posts, reports and blocks associated with the account, usage records, and known avatar and community-photo uploads.

Need help?

If you cannot access the app or your account, email support@sunsetchaser.app from the email address associated with your account. Include “Delete Sunset Chaser account” in the subject line. We may ask for information needed to verify that the request is yours.
